The demo — three acts

1

Accounts Payable — it reads, codes & posts AP

Invoices → drop INV-2043 → open it → Approve → Xero: Business ▸ Bills to pay ▸ Draft
SayIt doesn't just read the invoice — it codes every line to the right nominal from your Xero chart, then posts the bill. Nothing posts until someone approves.
Point at: the green Nominal column (AI-assigned, click to override) → then the matching bill in Xero. Show INV-3390 (IT kit) for coding variety.
2

Accounts Receivable — delivery note to invoice AR

Deliver to Invoice → upload DN-7001 → Xero: Business ▸ Invoices ▸ Draft
SayThe same engine runs in reverse. A signed delivery note comes back, and Xero raises the customer invoice automatically — order-to-cash, closed.
Point at: the read-back (customer, order ref, priced lines) → then the new draft sales invoice in Xero. The customer is matched or created automatically.
3

Trust it from day one — the audit trail Proof

Processed (sidebar) → copy a Ref → paste into Xero search
SayEarly on you won't fully trust a new system — so here's everything it has posted, each with its Xero reference. If it's ever unclear whether something went through, you check here. No black box.
Point at: the Ref under each status → search that ref in Xero → lands on the exact bill.
