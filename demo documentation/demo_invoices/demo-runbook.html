The walkthrough

seven phases, in order
1

Ledger-IQ — the accounting system

Set the scene: the finance system that owns suppliers & purchase orders.
  1. In the Ledger-IQ tab, sign in as the demo user.
  2. Open Suppliers (7 Highlands & Islands firms) and Purchase Orders (PO-10021–10025).
Say"This is the source of truth — who we buy from and what we've ordered."
2

Bulk onboarding by XML import

The new feature — stand up a client's reference data in one click.
  1. Go to Import in the sidebar.
  2. Customers: choose ledgeriq_customers.xml → Import → "Customers: 4 created." Show them on the Customers page.
  3. Products: choose ledgeriq_products.xml → Import → "Products: 5 created."
Say"Onboarding suppliers, customers and products is one XML import — and it's idempotent, so re-running just updates in place."
3

Invoice-IQ — connect & pull the POs

Prove the two apps are talking.
  1. Switch to the Invoice-IQ tab → Settings → Finance System (Ledger-IQ is selected).
  2. Click Test Connection → "Connected to Ledger-IQ successfully."
  3. Click Sync POs from Ledger-IQ → "Synced 5 POs."
Say"Invoice-IQ reads the open POs straight out of Ledger-IQ to match against."
4

Process the invoices

The core. On the Invoices page, drop each PDF into the upload zone — one at a time. Each auto-extracts, resolves the supplier in Ledger-IQ, and matches its PO.
Drop thisWhat happensFeature
INV-2043_Highland-Paper-Co.pdfMatched to PO-10021, high confidenceClean match
INV-9981_Islay-Logistics-Ltd.pdf“Total mismatch: Invoice £583.80 vs PO £528.00” + qty differencesDiscrepancy
INV-4410_Skye-Fresh-Produce.pdf“Supplier ‘Skye Fresh Produce’ not found in Ledger-IQ”Missing supplier
INV-3390_Grampian-IT-Solutions.pdf£4,200 > £1,000 → routed to Awaiting ApprovalApproval
Say at the mismatch"It caught that the invoice doesn't agree with the PO — it won't silently overpay."
Say at the missing supplier"That supplier isn't in the ledger yet, so it flags it instead of guessing — and you'd fix it live by importing Skye, re-syncing, and re-processing."
5

Approve → post back to Ledger-IQ

The round trip that ties the two apps together.
  1. Open the Highland (or Grampian) invoice → Approve.
  2. Flip to Ledger-IQ → Purchases — the bill is there, with its journal entry posted.
Say"Approve once in Invoice-IQ and it lands in the ledger as a purchase bill, bookkeeping done — no re-keying."
6

Remittances — the receivables side

The mirror image: a customer tells you which of your sales invoices they've paid, and Invoice-IQ marks them off. Same engine, opposite direction.
  1. In Invoice-IQ, open Remittances (sidebar) and drop Harbour Healthcare (North) Ltd Remittance Advice.pdf.
  2. Watch it flow Extracting → Posting → Posted — customer Harbour Healthcare, £1,144.68, 3 invoices matched (refs 1161 / 1162 / 1163).
  3. Flip to Ledger-IQ → Invoices: 1161 / 1162 / 1163 now show Paid, each with a customer receipt and journal posted.
Say"A customer's remittance comes in as a PDF, and the matching sales invoices are marked paid — receipt and bookkeeping posted, no manual reconciliation."
7

Land the story

Close on the whole loop in a sentence — then the punchline.
  1. Recap both directions: a supplier invoice paid, and a customer payment reconciled — all from PDFs.
  2. Add: "And this same flow works with QuickBooks, Sage or Xero — Ledger-IQ is just today's finance system."
The one-liner
A supplier PDF arrives — Invoice-IQ reads it, checks it against the purchase order and supplier list in Ledger-IQ, catches mismatches and unknown suppliers, routes the big ones for approval, and posts the bookkeeping straight into Ledger-IQ. A customer remittance arrives — the same engine marks the paid sales invoices off. Payables and receivables, both from PDFs.
Everything above runs on live production — invoice.sol-iq.co.uk and ledger.sol-iq.co.uk.

Reset between runs. To wipe pushed bills and restore the demo org, the repo has LedgerIQ/scripts/demo-clean-invoices.ts (removes Invoice-IQ-pushed bills) and demo-setup.ts (re-creates suppliers/POs, prints the API key). Ask and I'll fold these into a single "reset demo" command.
